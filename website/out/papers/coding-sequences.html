Part I

Coding Sequences as Executable Functions

A Type-Theoretic Framework for the Central Dogma

Matthew Long·YonedaAI Research Collective·March 2026·ProteinCode<T>

Abstract

We formalize protein-coding sequences as a typed compilation pipeline where the central dogma DNA→mRNA→Protein maps directly to Source→IR→Binary compilation. The codon table becomes an opcode mapping, reading frames correspond to parser state, alternative splicing produces dependent type families, and mutations are classified by their type-theoretic impact: silent (identity refactoring), missense (type-compatible substitution), nonsense (type error), and frameshift (parse error).
